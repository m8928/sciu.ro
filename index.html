Sciuro is a self-hosted, Rust-native log platform for teams that need familiar search and alerting workflows with direct control over their infrastructure.

What Sciuro does

Collect

Bring in logs through HEC-compatible HTTP, syslog, file, and Sciuro TCP inputs.

Search

Investigate events with SPL-style queries and a browser-based search interface.

Act

Run scheduled searches, configure alerts, and follow operational activity.

In development

Claude-assisted investigations

We are designing an assistant that proposes reviewable searches for incident questions and explains results with links to the underlying log events. The existing log engine provides the search and evidence layer; Claude integration is planned.
